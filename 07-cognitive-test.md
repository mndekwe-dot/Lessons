# 07 — Cognitive Assessment Prep

The cognitive test measures reasoning speed and accuracy, not programming. Typical sections:

## What to expect

1. **Numerical reasoning** — number sequences, percentages, ratios, quick arithmetic,
   reading small tables/charts.
2. **Logical / abstract reasoning** — "which figure comes next?", odd one out,
   pattern matrices (shapes rotating, alternating, growing).
3. **Verbal reasoning** — short passage + "True / False / Cannot say" statements.
4. **Attention / speed** — comparing strings or numbers for exact matches.

## How to practice (free)

- Search "free cognitive ability practice test" — good sources:
  - CodeSignal practice area (they may use CodeSignal for this too — the link in your email)
  - 123test.com (free IQ/cognitive tests)
  - JobTestPrep free samples, AssessmentDay free tests, Practice Aptitude Tests (practiceaptitudetests.com)
- Do **2–3 short timed tests per week** in the last two weeks. Speed comes from repetition.

## Skills to drill

**Number sequences** — always check, in this order:
1. Constant difference? (2, 5, 8, 11 → +3)
2. Growing difference? (1, 2, 4, 7, 11 → +1, +2, +3...)
3. Multiplication? (3, 6, 12, 24 → ×2)
4. Alternating pattern? (1, 10, 2, 20, 3, ...) — look at every 2nd element
5. Squares/cubes/primes? (1, 4, 9, 16 / 2, 3, 5, 7, 11)

**Fast percentage tricks:**
- 10% = divide by 10; 5% = half of that; 1% = divide by 100 — build any % from these
- x% of y = y% of x (8% of 25 = 25% of 8 = 2 — much easier!)
- Increase by 20% = ×1.2; decrease by 20% = ×0.8

**Ratio problems:** total = sum of ratio parts. Ratio 3:2 with total 40 → parts of 8 → 24 and 16.

**"True / False / Cannot say":** use ONLY the passage. Real-world knowledge is a trap —
if the passage doesn't state it or directly imply it, the answer is **Cannot say**.

## Test-day tips

- These tests are usually designed so most people **don't finish** — accuracy on the
  questions you attempt beats rushing through everything.
- Don't get stuck: 30–60 seconds max per question, guess and move on (check whether
  wrong answers are penalized; if not, never leave blanks).
- Sleep well the night before — cognitive scores drop measurably when tired. Arrive early;
  it's in person, so plan transport to the venue in advance.
