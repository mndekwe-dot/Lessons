# 08 — Application Video & Profile Guide

In the application form for **The Gym**, you must:
1. **Record a short video of yourself** (verifying your English proficiency and communication).
2. **Provide your CodeWars profile link** (showcasing simple algorithm katas solved).
3. **Share background information about yourself.**

This guide gives you an exact, word-for-word spoken script and setup instructions to make a great impression.

---

## 1. Video Recording Script (60 to 90 seconds)

Practice reading this out loud several times until it sounds natural, confident, and smooth.

```
"Hello everyone!

My name is [Your Full Name], and I am currently a student at [Your University], studying [Your Department / Course, e.g., Computer Science / Information Technology / Software Engineering].

I am passionate about software engineering and problem solving. Over the past months, I have been actively building my programming foundations, working with Python and JavaScript, and solving algorithm challenges on CodeWars.

I am applying to The Gym because I believe in hands-on, practical software development. I am really drawn to The Gym's methodology — collaborating in teams of five, receiving weekly mentorship from industry coaches, and working on real digital projects.

I am fully prepared and committed to dedicate the required 20 to 25 hours every week alongside my university studies. I manage my time with discipline, and I treat this traineeship as a top priority for my career.

Thank you very much for this opportunity and for considering my application. I look forward to the assessment stage!"
```

---

## 2. Tips for Recording a Great Video

### Visuals & Lighting:
- **Eye level:** Place your phone or laptop on books so the camera is at eye level (not looking up your nose).
- **Light in front:** Sit facing a window or lamp. Never have a bright window behind you, or your face will appear dark.
- **Background:** Choose a clean, quiet space (a plain wall, a neat room, or a study desk).

### Audio & Speech:
- **Quiet room:** Close doors and windows to minimize street noise.
- **Pacing:** Do not rush. Speak at a calm, conversational speed.
- **Smile and make eye contact:** Look directly into the camera lens, not at your own face on the screen.

## 3. CodeWars Profile Checklist

Before submitting your application form, ensure your CodeWars profile is ready:

1. **Complete at least 5 to 10 katas:**
   - Solve the 8 kyu katas in [`06-codewars-kata-playbook.md`](06-codewars-kata-playbook.md) and [`practice/codewars_practice.js`](practice/codewars_practice.js).
   - Recommended first katas on [codewars.com](https://www.codewars.com/):
     - *Multiply* (8 kyu)
     - *Even or Odd* (8 kyu)
     - *Opposite number* (8 kyu)
     - *Return Negative* (8 kyu)
     - *Sum of positive* (8 kyu)
     - *String repeat* (8 kyu)
     - *Remove First and Last Character* (8 kyu)
     - *Square(n) Sum* (8 kyu)
     - *Find the smallest integer in the array* (8 kyu)
     - *Vowel Count* (7 kyu)
2. **Set your primary language to JavaScript** in your profile settings.
3. **Copy your profile URL:**
   - Go to `codewars.com/users/YOUR_USERNAME`.
   - Copy the URL and paste it into the application form.
